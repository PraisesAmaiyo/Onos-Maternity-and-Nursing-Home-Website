About Onos Maternity and Nursing Home

Compassionate patient care, maternal wellbeing and professional nursing support at every stage of care.

Onos Maternity and Nursing Home is a healthcare facility dedicated to quality maternity and nursing care in a safe, supportive and welcoming environment.

We provide maternal health support, antenatal and postnatal care, nursing services, patient monitoring and general healthcare assistance.

We serve individuals and families with attention, respect and dedication to their health and wellbeing.

Our services

Care that follows you from your first antenatal visit through recovery and beyond.

  • Maternal health support

    Guidance and care that protect your health and comfort before, during and after pregnancy.

  • Antenatal care

    Regular check-ups and advice to keep you and your baby well through pregnancy.

  • Postnatal care

    Support for your recovery and your newborn in the weeks after delivery.

  • Nursing services

    Skilled, attentive nursing care from a professional and caring team.

  • Patient monitoring

    Close observation of your condition so changes are noticed and handled early.

  • General healthcare assistance

    Everyday health support for individuals and families.
